# Handwritten Results Transcription Note

The table in [raw_results.csv](raw_results.csv) is a direct transcription of the supplied handwritten photograph. It records three columns headed, in French, as completed parts, mean queue length, and mean utilization, grouped by mean service time 3, 4, and 4.5.

## Scope of validation

The transcription was checked for:

- five rows in every service-time group;
- 15 rows overall;
- numerical consistency between the summary and CSV;
- utilization values within the valid 0–1 range; and
- agreement between the three headings and the chart expressions found in the model source.

The photograph is not included as a result artifact because it is source evidence rather than an exported simulation output. If any digit in the notebook needs correction, edit the CSV first and then regenerate the summary and chart.
